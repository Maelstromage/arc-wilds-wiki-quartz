# Red Palm Goblins

- A goblin clan active around [[Cloven Hoof Cave]] in the earliest surviving notes. [[Session 01]]
- Their sign is described as a black backdrop marked with a red goblin handprint. [[Session 01]]
- Associated with goblin patrols around the cave entrances. [[Session 01]]
- At least some members kept watch outside while a larger, better-armored goblin with a wolf moved in and out of the cave. [[Session 01]]
- Their presence in the earliest surviving notes suggests Cloven Hoof Cave was not merely an old tomb but an actively occupied goblin site
