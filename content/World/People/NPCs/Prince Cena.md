# Prince Cena

- Prince of Sinte
- Arrived in [[Ferris]] to purchase the city on behalf of his father's religious empire. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Received word in Ferris that his father had died, making him heir to the crown and effectively king, though earlier records in the shelves used the spelling "Sina." [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Striking, battle-hardened, and extremely difficult to read emotionally
- Maintained near-perfect composure even upon learning of his father's death. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Claimed he does not want to be king. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Said his father was a crusader and implied he did not wish to continue the same path. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Distrusted the soldiers sent to escort him home, believing many were mercenaries rather than loyal retainers. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Suspected figures named Galley and Sarah of arranging matters so that he might never safely return to Sinte. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Offered the party magical rewards and passage into Sinte in exchange for loyal escort service. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Independently spiked his own soldiers' drinks with nightshade after concluding they could not be trusted. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Agreed to cooperate more openly with the party after discovering they had secretly pursued the same plan. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Personally accompanied the party to confront Ferris's mayor and later joined the attack on the mercenary encampment outside town. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Demonstrated calculated charm and ruthless precision by lulling the mercenary captain into ease before beheading him under cover of [[Watsie]]'s silence spell. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Fought efficiently through both the camp assault and the later shadow attack in the grove. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Appears capable of being sincere, strategic, and terrifying all at once; whether he is a reformer, a trapped heir, or simply a colder liar than most remains unsettled in the record.
