# Bartholomew

- Twin brother of Barnabas, arriving too late to save him during the night assault on the mercenary encampment outside [[Ferris]]. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Witnessed a mercenary holding Barnabas's severed head aloft and nearly roused the whole camp with his grief-stricken scream. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Turned that grief into immediate vengeance, helping the party destroy the remaining mercenaries. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Punched holes in a massive oil barrel and hurled it into the advancing enemy before fire ignited it. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Delivered Barnabas's eulogy at the funeral pyre and pledged his life to the group, swearing to finish what his brother had started. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Later helped save the party in the mysterious grove by blasting paths through spirit rattlesnakes and stuffing incapacitated companions into a magical handy haversack to carry them to safety. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Entered the record as a stranger made companion in the span of a single terrible night.
