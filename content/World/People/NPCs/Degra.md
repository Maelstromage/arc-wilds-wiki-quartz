# Degra

- Elven druid active in [[Ferris]]
- Part of the local resistance to [[Prince Cena]]'s attempted purchase of the city on behalf of Sinte. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Handed out "Save Ferris" flyers to rally support against Sinte's growing control. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Claimed that Sinte had already swallowed her own hometown and driven out druids and others outside the empire's faith. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Warned the party about Ferris's political danger soon after their arrival in the city. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Helped the party by providing nightshade seeds used to drug Prince Cena's escort. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- Was entrusted with the seized gold chest for distribution to Ferris's citizens. [[session-2026-05-13]]
- May remain an important local ally or witness if the party returns to Ferris
