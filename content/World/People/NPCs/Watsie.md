# Watsie

- Bodyguard and close operational ally of [[Prince Cena]]
- First clearly named in the Ferris crisis during the assault on the mercenary encampment outside town. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Introduced herself to the party at the military camp while keeping a ready hand near her sword. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- A capable warrior and magic user who masked key moments of the attack with repeated castings of magical silence. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Helped Prince Cena execute the mercenary captain by dropping a silence field before the killing stroke. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Fought efficiently through the camp assault, decapitating enemies with swift, disciplined strikes. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Also joined the later battle in the grove, where she helped illuminate and cut down the shadow entities. [[session-2026-05-20]]
- Appears composed, vigilant, and dangerous in the quiet way that usually means someone else bleeds first.
