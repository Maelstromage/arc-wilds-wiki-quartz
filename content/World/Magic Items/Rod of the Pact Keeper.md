# Rod of the Pact Keeper

- A magical rod found near [[Myeve]] after the party escaped the trials of [[Magic Tower]]
- Floated above the ground amid purple tentacles and the manifestation of a vast many-eyed entity
- Psychically linked to a being that demanded a champion to help bring about the end of all time
- Attempts to learn or speak the associated entity's name caused mental strain or catatonia
- When seized, the rod bonded violently with its bearer through writhing tentacles
- Its bonding transformed one party member from a barbarian into a warlock
- Functions as a warlock's rod of power and can restore expended magical strength
- Appears to carry an ongoing curse, influence, or eldritch bond beyond its ordinary magical benefits
