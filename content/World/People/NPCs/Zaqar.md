# Zaqar

- A strange extraplanar being encountered through a hole in the wall inside [[Magic Tower]]
- Associated with an impossible void of stars, nebulae, drifting stone, a distant castle, and enormous tentacles moving through an unreal sea
- Spoke to each member of the party from directly behind them no matter which way they turned
- Appeared at first as a tiny marble or sphere that could swell to an enormous size and then shrink away again
- Claimed the party existed on a lower plane than its own
- Spoke in riddles and paradoxes, especially about moving forward to go backward and backward to go forward
- Capable of pulling creatures briefly into its own reality and warping their bodies in disturbing ways
- Seemed curious rather than openly malicious
- Remains one of the stranger entities known to be tied to [[Magic Tower]]
