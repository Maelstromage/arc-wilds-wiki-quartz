# Amanda

- Pale, gothic-looking woman tied to death, spirits, and the passage of souls
- A servant of [[Myrkul]]
- First encountered near [[Magic Tower]], where she wore black and carried a necromantic ankh necklace
- Seemed surprised that the party could see her at all
- The ankh was stolen from her, after which she vanished
- Later found within [[Magic Tower]] in the form of a small amnesiac lizard
- Carried instructions in Primordial that, when read aloud, restored both her true form and her relic
- Explained that she had been cursed into lizard form after losing the relic
- Floated upward through the tower after being restored in order to escort a death-cheating wizard to the underworld
- Her exact present status remains unknown, but she appears to have resumed her role as a guide of souls
