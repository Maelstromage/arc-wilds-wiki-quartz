## Rumors
- There is a settlement tied to the Knights of the Holy Order.

- Flyers have been placed to spread word of it.
- The earliest surviving notes describe it as a new human settlement. [[Session 01]]

## What is Known
- Knights of the Holy Order Settlement is a known settlement.
- It is tied to Chauntea, goddess of agriculture.
- The settlement is known at least in part through flyers that have been posted.
- The earliest surviving notes describe its people as very devout. [[Session 01]]
- The earliest surviving notes also suggest they were looking for miners to convert. [[Session 01]]

## History
- The settlement exists as part of the influence of the Knights of the Holy Order.
- The earliest surviving notes describe it as a new human settlement emerging in the region. [[Session 01]]
- Its fuller history has not yet been recorded.
