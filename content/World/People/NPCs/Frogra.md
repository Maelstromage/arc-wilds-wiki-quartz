# Frogra

- A Beholder living in a muddy pit on the road near [[Myeve]]
- Introduced himself as Frogra
- Initially hostile, chomped through a rescue rope, and fired eye rays at the party when disturbed
- Wants minions and had apparently been waiting for people to fall into his pit
- Claimed that his father never loved him
- Claimed he accidentally disintegrated his only friend
- Emotionally needy, lonely, and prone to using oddly modern therapeutic language when he feels ignored or invalidated
- Negotiated with the party rather than fighting to the death
- Released them after receiving a promise that they would bring him twenty stronger minions
- Later shared a campfire with the party and may become an unlikely future ally or complication
- Vanished when a white beam transported the party from their roadside camp to [[Ferris]] [[session-2026-05-13]]
- His current whereabouts are unknown
