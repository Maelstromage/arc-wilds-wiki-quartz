# Location: Magic Tower

## Rumors
- A wizard's tower once stood on the outskirts of town but now hangs inside a cloud high above the ground.
- Those who have looked into it speak of bizarre, dangerous floors and strange magical tests.
- Some say the wizard inside is still alive because the tower continues to move and persist.
- Strange beings, impossible spaces, and moral trials are all said to lie somewhere within.

## What is Known
- Magic Tower is a wizard's tower on the outskirts of town.
- It no longer rests on the ground where it once stood.
- The tower now floats within a dense, stationary cloud high above the land, casting a shadow below.
- The original ground site remains as a cleared circular plot with a cobblestone path and the faint impression of a foundation.
- The tower contains multiple dangerous and unusual floors or chambers that seem designed as trials.
- Known features inside the tower include a life-size magical chessboard, a flooded upper passage inhabited by sharks, a locked chamber of glowing vials and goblets, a suit of armor that mirrors intruders, a room filled with squirrels, and a congregation of thirteen masked figures in black robes.
- The magical chessboard can pull participants into the pieces themselves, and losing can leave them unconscious when cast back out.
- One chamber contains a small hole in the wall that opens into an impossible extradimensional void of stars, nebulae, a floating rock, a distant castle, and enormous tentacles moving through an unreal sea.
- Beyond that opening lies a reality-warping space tied to [[Zaqar]], where gravity, distance, and time do not behave normally.
- The tower also contains a teleportation sigil that can lead not to safety, but to further tests.
- One such trial space was a mirrored chamber that forced isolated moral choices and acts of self-sacrifice.
- A small lizard found within the tower was later revealed to be [[Amanda]], a servant of [[Myrkul]] who had been cursed after losing her relic.
- After Amanda was restored, she ascended deeper into the tower to escort a death-cheating wizard to the underworld.
- Water, sharks, and debris later fell from above, suggesting a violent or catastrophic end to whatever remained in the higher levels.

## History
- The party traveled to the tower site after John Harrison directed them there to learn more about a growing magical quartz cluster.
- On arrival, they discovered the tower had risen into the sky and was now enclosed within a cloud.
- A nearby farmer confirmed the tower had been there for some time.
- The party first sent Jeremy to scout the interior and judged the place too dangerous to assault at that time.
- A later expedition entered the tower directly, overcame the magical chessboard, encountered Amanda's curse, breached the extradimensional chamber linked to Zaqar, and survived a sequence of moral trials tied to the tower's escape sigil.

## People

### [[Amanda]]
- A servant of Myrkul who was found in cursed lizard form within the tower and later restored.

### [[Zaqar]]
- A curious and deeply unnatural extraplanar being associated with the impossible space accessible through the hole in the wall.
